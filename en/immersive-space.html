A space imagined from the painting.

  • Sound: a gentle background accompanies the space. Use the button at the top to turn it on or off.
  • On iPhone: drag the view to look, use the arrows to move and + / − to rise or descend.
  • On a computer: drag with the mouse; use W A S D or the arrows, and E / Q to rise or descend.

How to explore

  • Look: drag the space with a finger or mouse.
  • Move: hold the on-screen arrows, or use W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Rise and descend: use + / − on screen, or E / Q on the keyboard.
  • Keyboard only: use J / L to turn and I / K to look up or down.

This environment is an experimental interpretation of the painting. You can move freely through its forms.
