Montico's planet

A small world without edges, where lunar terrain meets the colours, marks and materials of the artworks. Sixteen regions blend into each other.

Keep travelling in one direction and a complete circuit takes about two minutes. The planet is a freely imagined miniature.

  • On iPhone: drag to look, hold the arrows to move. Use + / − to rise or descend.
  • On a computer: drag with the mouse and use W A S D or the arrows. Hold Shift to go faster.
  • Artworks: discover the images inspiring the region you are in.

How to explore

  • Look: drag with a finger or the mouse.
  • Move: hold the arrows on screen or use W A S D or the keyboard arrows. Release to stop.
  • Rise and descend: use + / − or E / Q. Rise to see the curvature of the planet.
  • Keyboard only: J / L turn; I / K look up and down.

This imaginary planet is a tribute assembled from 32 artwork images. The three-dimensional forms freely reinterpret the materials and sculptures at Casa Montico; they are not scans of the artworks.

Starting terrain: LRO/LOLA data, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit. Scale and relief have been artistically adapted.
