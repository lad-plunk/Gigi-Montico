The planet, a laboratory of ideas

Nine ideas to try: marks become forms, matter separates, a journey inside wood, living landscapes and changing sounds.

The Map takes you directly to each experiment. The sky changes with your position: day, sunset, night and dawn. Shadows are lightweight approximations.

  • iPhone: drag to look and hold the arrows to move.
  • Computer: mouse and W A S D or arrow keys. Release to stop.
  • After your visit: complete the questionnaire.

Map of the nine ideas

Select an idea to visit it immediately, or start a slow circuit. You can stop at any time.

How to explore

  • Look: drag with a finger or the mouse.
  • Move: hold the screen arrows or use W A S D or the keyboard arrows. Release to stop.
  • Rise and descend: use + / − or E / Q. J / L turn; I / K look up and down.
  • Experiments: use the idea button at the top left. The map takes you directly to each idea.

A circuit takes about two minutes. The sky depends on position; shadows are lightweight approximations. Sculptures are interpretations, not scans.

Terrain: NASA CGI Moon Kit, LRO/LOLA. Wood photograph: Casa Montico.

Music: That Zen Moment, by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0; ambient layers are generated in the browser.
