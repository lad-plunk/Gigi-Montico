How to explore

  • Look: drag with a finger or mouse.
  • Move: hold the on-screen arrows or use W A S D or the arrow keys. Release to stop.
  • Rise and descend: use + / − on screen or E / Q on the keyboard.
  • Keyboard only: use J / L to turn and I / K to look up or down.
