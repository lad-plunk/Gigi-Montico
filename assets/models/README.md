# Source models for the Deserto rosso experiments

All three files are the original GLB downloads, retained unchanged. The viewer reads their geometry and applies a texture derived from Gigi Montico's painting. The dune and lunar surfaces are sampled into smoothed, joined height grids, then repeated and enlarged to create walkable spaces. The dune relief is also flattened vertically. These are artistic interpretations, not surveys or scans of a specific landscape.

Grab3D reports that its models were built procedurally using scripts developed with AI assistance. 3DAssets.dev identifies its regolith model as made with AI tools. The website adds its own texture, light, atmospheric particles and animated painting-like forms.
