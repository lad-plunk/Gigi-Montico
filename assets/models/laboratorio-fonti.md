# Planet laboratory: sources and limits

This is a separate experimental version of the Montico planet. It reuses the existing lunar height map and the two artwork atlases described in [pianeta-fonti.md](pianeta-fonti.md). It does not modify the original planet or the earlier three landscapes.

The curved wooden forms and the walkable imagined interior use a detail sampled from `assets/images/casa-montico/casa-montico-001.jpg`. Their geometry is invented from the available photograph and is not a photogrammetric reconstruction or scan. The original photograph is available at the sculpture stop. The collage and avant-garde note is an invitation to discuss ideas with the artist and makes no claim about specific historical influences on his work.

The sky is a procedural day-to-night gradient driven by position on the sphere. Daytime contact shadows are translucent approximations, not calculated shadow maps. Small pieces of geometry move, scatter or reassemble. The location-dependent ambient layer is generated locally over the existing `That Zen Moment` recording, credited in the experience.

The associated bilingual questionnaire is a static client-side form. Draft responses are stored locally when browser storage is available; the site does not send or collect them. The visitor explicitly generates and copies a summary to share elsewhere.
