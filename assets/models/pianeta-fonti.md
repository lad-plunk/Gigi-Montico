# Il pianeta di Montico: source notes

The globe is a freely imagined tribute, not a scientific Moon model or a scan of Montico's sculptures. Its 600 m circumference and 5 m/s walking speed give a nominal two-minute great-circle circuit. Elevation is artistically exaggerated and the landscape includes additional invented craters. The visitor can move over the poles and around the whole sphere.

The underlying global height field is derived from the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), `ldem_4_uint.tif`, based on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter LOLA measurements. The build script normalizes the 16-bit elevation data to 8-bit, resizes it to 512 × 256 and creates the compact `assets/images/pianeta-rilievo-lunare.png` height map. The renderer creates a three-dimensional spherical mesh from that height map and adds invented crater detail. NASA SVS credits Ernie Wright for the visualization; source data are NASA/LRO/LOLA.

The two 2048 × 2048 JPEG atlases contain details from 32 images already on this website, including works from the surreal-watercolour collection. Their complete paths and the available titles are listed in `assets/data/pianeta-works.js`. Photographic-archive and watercolour images without reliable titles are identified by their existing GM or AS IDs rather than invented titles. Sculptural forms are new procedural interpretations inspired by the materials and works shown in [Casa Montico](../../it/casa-montico.html), including the wooden forms in `assets/images/casa-montico/casa-montico-001.jpg` and assemblages in `casa-montico-025.jpg` and `casa-montico-040.jpg`. They are not replicas or scans.

Run `python 10_Working/build_montico_planet_assets.py` from any directory to rebuild the two atlases, lunar map and artwork metadata. It reads the existing site media and downloads the public NASA TIFF; it does not overwrite source artworks. NumPy and Pillow are required. The viewer also reuses the locally hosted and credited music already used in the other immersive examples.
