From 1961 to today

A life devoted to painting

From Milan to the Lodi countryside, Gigi Montico has built a continuous professional practice through exhibitions, commissioned works, school projects, research and books.

The move from Milan to Dovera did not interrupt the career. It changed its context, bringing the practice closer to local communities and new forms of research.
